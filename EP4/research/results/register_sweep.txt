# register_sweep.py
# 2026-11-22 16:21 on Welton, engine code 72b86b63cbf7, Build cuda_13.3.r13.3/compiler.38244171_0

Cap the registers (-maxrregcount) and measure occupancy and speed. The caps are interleaved (forward,
then backward) so a clock that drifts during the sweep hits every cap alike; speed is also given in
cycles of the slowest SM, which do not depend on the clock.
