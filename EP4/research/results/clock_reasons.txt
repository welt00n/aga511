Found on 2026-11-22: FP64, and FP32 on 1 SM, run at ~2880-2895 MHz, held by 'reliability' (the voltage
ceiling). FP32 on 36 SMs hits the software power cap: the enforced limit moves between 85 and 115 W
(Dynamic Boost) and the clock follows it down. Register spills are not the cause.
