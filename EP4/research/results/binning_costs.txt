2/nμ of μ), each projected along its own direction (engine/physics/photon.cuh). R = size/2 pixels.

Found on 2026-11-22: at 1024², 300 frames, nμ 95 the blur is ~11 px along the rotation and at the equator,
but the polar bin spans 0–11.8° (up to 105 px). Equal-μ bins are equal in photons, not in angle. Keeping the
blur in pixels while doubling the size costs 16× the photons (N ∝ nμ · frames · size²).
