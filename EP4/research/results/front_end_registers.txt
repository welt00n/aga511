Why does sm_80 need more registers than sm_120? Separate the front end (C++ -> PTX, NVVM) from the
back end (PTX -> SASS, ptxas): compile the PTX for each compute_XX and assemble it for each SM.

Found on 2026-11-22: the jump is at compute_100. PTX for compute_75...90 needs 96-98 registers (FP64),
compute_100...120 80-84, whatever SM ptxas assembles it for. CUDA 13 uses a newer NVVM (LLVM 18) for
Blackwell and later; its PTX has fewer branches and more selects. ptxas adds only ~2.
