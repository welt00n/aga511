What does a register cap do to the kernel's code? The machine instructions of trace_photons (static: in the
code, not executed) for each -maxrregcount cap: all of them without the NOPs, and the spill stores and loads
(STL / LDL: local memory, which is private to the thread but lives in device memory, cached by L1 and L2).
