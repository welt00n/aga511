EP1 question 3: pi with 14 decimals (error 1e-14)
  NEVER with FP32: FP32 keeps about 7 significant digits, so its needle test can never support 14 decimals. The numbers below are what it would take if the error kept falling.
  Needles needed: (5.4 ± 0.4)e28 = (k / 1e-14)^2 (the free fit would say 2.3e+28: its slope is off by 0.009, stretched over 18 powers of ten)
  Speed of this GPU (all SMs): (1.0735 ± 0.0035)e11 needles/s (statistical) ± 0.6% (systematic, clock); 9.26e+10 counting the serial part, at N = 1.0e+11
  Time: (5.1 ± 0.4)e17 s = 1.6e+10 years ± 8%
  For N this large the serial part is nothing, so p -> 1 and time falls as 1/n (Amdahl):
  in 80 years you would need 2.0e+08 GPUs like this one (7.2e+09 SMs)
  N = 5.4e+28 does not fit in a 64-bit integer (max 9.2e18): it can only be extrapolated.
